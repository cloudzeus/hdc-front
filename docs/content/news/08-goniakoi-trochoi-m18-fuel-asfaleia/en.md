**Short answer:** On an M18 FUEL cordless angle grinder, safety comes down to the RAPIDSTOP disc brake, AUTOSTOP for bind-ups, the type of switch and protection against unintended start-up. According to Milwaukee, the M18 FSAG125XB stops the disc in under 2 seconds and the M18 FSAGSVO125X safety grinder in under 1 second.

The angle grinder works every day on building sites and in workshops, and it does not forgive carelessness. In recent years cordless grinders have gained safety functions that reduce the risk. It pays to know what each one does before you choose.

## Why does an angle grinder need extra care?

The disc spins at thousands of revolutions per minute, a few centimetres from your hands. Every tradesperson knows the risks:

- the disc **binds** in the cut and the grinder jerks or twists in your hand,
- the disc **keeps spinning** after you release the switch,
- the grinder **starts unexpectedly**, for example when the battery goes in,
- the disc **breaks** and fragments fly off.

Each safety function described below answers one of these risks.

## What is RAPIDSTOP and how fast does the disc stop?

RAPIDSTOP is the disc brake. Without a brake, a disc keeps spinning for a while after you release the switch. In that time it can touch the floor, an extension lead or your clothing.

According to Milwaukee, the M18 FSAG125XB brakes the disc in under 2 seconds. The M18 FSAGSVO125X, which Milwaukee calls a "safety grinder", stops it in under 1 second.

## What does AUTOSTOP do when the disc binds?

A bind-up happens when the disc gets pinched in the cut, usually because the workpiece closes up or the grinder tilts. The disc stops abruptly and the full force of the motor turns the tool body in your hands.

On the M18 FSAGSVO125X, AUTOSTOP shuts the tool down when it detects a bind-up or an unnatural movement. The difference from RAPIDSTOP: the brake engages when you release the switch, while AUTOSTOP steps in on its own, when you have no time to react.

## What do dual triggers and a deadman switch mean?

The M18 FSAGSVO125X has **dual-trigger activation**: to run, both of your hands must be on the tool. If one hand comes off, the grinder stops. The switches are **deadman** type, which means they cannot be locked on. The moment you let go, the tool switches off.

The M18 FSAG125XB has a **slide switch**, a layout many tradespeople are used to. On this model Milwaukee lists protection against automatic start-up, covered below.

## What else should you check on a cordless grinder?

### What is line lock-out (protection against automatic start-up)?

On the M18 FSAG125XB, Milwaukee lists a function that prevents automatic start-up. In practice, if the switch has been left in the on position, the grinder should not start by itself when you click in a battery. You reset the switch first, then switch on again.

### What does soft start do?

With soft start the motor builds up speed gradually. The tool does not give your hand a sudden jolt when you switch it on. It is listed on the M18 FSAG125XB.

### What is anti-kickback protection?

Kickback is the sudden jerk of the grinder towards the user when the disc catches. According to Milwaukee, the M18 FSAG125XB has anti-kickback protection. No function, though, replaces a firm grip.

### Why does a non-removable guard matter?

The guard protects you from sparks and from disc fragments if the disc breaks. The M18 FSAG125XB has a 125 mm guard that adjusts without a key. On the M18 FSAGSVO125X the guard cannot be removed at all, but it adjusts without tools.

### What does an anti-vibration side handle offer?

Less vibration means better control. The M18 FSAG125XB has an anti-vibration side handle. The M18 FSAGSVO125X has a non-removable side handle with 15 positions, and Milwaukee lists 3.27 m/s² vibration in surface grinding, the lowest in its class according to the company.

### What is FIXTEC?

FIXTEC lets you change discs without a key. Both grinders have it. When changing discs is easy, you are more likely to fit the right disc for each material instead of making do with whatever is already mounted.

## How do you work safely with an angle grinder?

However good the grinder, safety starts with habits. A short checklist before you pull the trigger:

1. **Personal protective equipment.** Safety glasses or a face shield, gloves, hearing protection. See our [head and face protection](/en/katalogos/prostasia-kefalis-prosopou) and [work gloves](/en/katalogos/prostasia-ano-akron).
2. **Guard in the right position.** It must sit between you and the disc. Never remove it.
3. **The right disc for the material.** Different for cutting metal, grinding or stone. See our [angle grinder discs](/en/katalogos/metallikoi-diskoi-goniakon-trochon).
4. **Check the disc's speed rating.** The disc's maximum speed must cover the grinder's maximum speed.
5. **Check for cracks.** A chipped or cracked disc goes in the bin.
6. **Secure the workpiece.** Clamp it, do not hold it with your other hand.
7. **Both hands on the tool,** with the side handle fitted.
8. **Let the disc stop** before putting the grinder down, and remove the battery before changing discs.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is RAPIDSTOP on Milwaukee angle grinders?
It is the disc brake. As soon as you let go of the switch, the disc stops quickly instead of spinning freely. According to Milwaukee, the M18 FSAG125XB stops the disc in under 2 seconds and the M18 FSAGSVO125X in under 1 second.

### What is the difference between RAPIDSTOP and AUTOSTOP?
RAPIDSTOP brakes the disc when you release the switch. AUTOSTOP acts without any input from you: it shuts the tool down when the disc binds or the grinder makes an unnatural movement. Milwaukee lists both on the M18 FSAGSVO125X.

### Does the M18 FSAG125XB have AUTOSTOP?
AUTOSTOP is not listed in Milwaukee's data for the M18 FSAG125XB. What is listed is anti-kickback protection, soft start, protection against automatic start-up and the RAPIDSTOP brake. AUTOSTOP is listed on the M18 FSAGSVO125X safety grinder.

### Can I take the guard off to work more comfortably?
No. The guard is the only thing between you and a disc that can break. Adjust its position, never remove it. On the M18 FSAGSVO125X the guard cannot be removed at all and is adjusted without tools.

### What speed rating does my disc need?
The maximum permitted speed marked on the disc must be equal to or higher than the grinder's maximum speed. According to Milwaukee, both grinders in this article reach 8,500 rpm. If the marking is missing or unreadable, do not use the disc.
