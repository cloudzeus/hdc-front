**Short answer:** PACKOUT is Milwaukee's modular storage and transport system. Tool boxes, organisers, drawer boxes and a trolley box lock together into a single stack that rolls as one unit. According to Milwaukee, the trolley box carries up to 113 kg, and the boxes feature an IP65 seal and metal-reinforced corners.

Every tradesperson knows the scene: five mismatched boxes in the back of the van, three trips up to the third floor and an organiser that popped open on the stairs. PACKOUT is built to fix exactly that. It is not just a tool box but a system that grows with your work.

## What does "modular" storage actually mean?

Modular means every piece is designed to connect with the rest. In PACKOUT, tool boxes, organisers and totes lock onto one another, even when they have different footprints. Milwaukee describes it as a multi-footprint system.

In practice the routine becomes simple:

- build the stack once, in the workshop or the van,
- roll it on the trolley box to where you are working,
- unlatch only the box you need at that moment.

## Which pieces make up the PACKOUT range?

The range is large, but it falls into a few families:

- **Trolley box:** the base of the stack, with wheels and a pull handle.
- **Tool boxes:** in compact, standard, large and extra-large sizes, plus a 3-piece set (trolley, large and standard).
- **Drawer boxes:** with 2, 3, 4 or 2+1 drawers.
- **Organisers:** for screws, anchors, terminals and small parts.
- **Soft bags:** for the day's hand tools.
- **Racks, bases, wall mounts, hooks and bins:** for the van and the workshop.
- **Coolers and tumblers:** for water and food on site.
- **PACKOUT-compatible tools and devices:** such as the [PACKOUT M18 6-port charger](/en/proion/fortistis-6-thyron-packout-m18-4932480162-4932480162), the PACKOUT radio/charger or the MXF TLIC-601 site light.

## What can the PACKOUT trolley box handle?

The [PACKOUT trolley box](/en/proion/troley-metaforas-packout-4932464078-4932464078) is the piece that carries the whole stack. According to Milwaukee:

- it has a load capacity of up to **113 kg**,
- it rolls on **228 mm** all-terrain wheels,
- it has an industrial-grade extension handle,
- the shell is made of impact-resistant polymers,
- it has an **IP65** weather seal, metal-reinforced corners and a metal-reinforced locking point,
- it includes an organiser tray and a mount for the ONE-KEY TICK tracker,
- its outer dimensions are 530 × 561 × 472 mm (H × W × D).

Big wheels matter on gravel, on rubble and on stairs. That is where a trolley with small castors gets stuck.

## What does IP65 mean in practice?

An IP code shows how well an enclosure is protected against dust and water. The first digit refers to dust, the second to water.

- **6 (dust):** the box is dust-tight. Ideal for renovation, cutting and sanding work.
- **5 (water):** it withstands water jets from any direction, so it can take a downpour on site.

IP65 does not mean the box can stay submerged in water. For puddles and flooded basements, keep it off the ground.

## When is a PACKOUT drawer box worth it?

A drawer box is the answer for items you reach for all the time and do not want to unstack. According to Milwaukee:

- it comes in 2-, 3-, 4- and 2+1-drawer versions,
- each drawer runs on steel ball-bearing slides and holds up to **11 kg**,
- the adjustable Quick-Adjust dividers split each drawer the way you want,
- a locking bar keeps the drawers shut in transit,
- it can be secured with a padlock,
- the drawers open even with boxes stacked on top.

For small parts you want to see at a glance, pair it with a [PACKOUT organiser](/en/proion/tabakiera-packout-4932464082-4932464082).

## Where does the ONE-KEY TICK tracker go?

The PACKOUT trolley box and tool boxes have a dedicated mounting spot for the ONE-KEY TICK tracker. ONE-KEY is Milwaukee's free cloud platform for tool inventory and tracking. With a TICK in place, the tool box itself enters your digital inventory, not just the tools inside it.

## How should I build a PACKOUT stack for my trade?

There is no single right stack. The combinations below are suggestions to get you started.

### Electrician
Trolley box at the base, a drawer box above it for terminals, connectors and fuses, and an organiser on top for anchors and cable ties. A standard tool box for pliers, screwdrivers and testers.

### Plumber
Trolley box with a large tool box for wrenches, pipe cutters and power tools. An organiser on top for fittings, seals and pipe clips. Here IP65 matters, because the job often involves water.

### Installer (HVAC, windows and doors, drywall)
A large tool box for the drill, impact driver and batteries, a standard box for hand tools and an organiser for screws and plugs. In the van, the PACKOUT charger keeps the batteries ready.

## How do I organise the van and the workshop with PACKOUT?

Racks, bases and wall mounts turn PACKOUT into more than a stack. In the van, keep only the day's stack on the trolley and the other boxes locked onto the racking. In the workshop, the same boxes hang on the wall and become your storage shelving.

Two practical tips: heavy at the bottom so the stack does not tip, and a label on every box so everyone in the workshop finds what they need.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do all PACKOUT pieces lock together?
Yes, that is the core idea of the system. Milwaukee designs PACKOUT tool boxes, organisers and totes to lock onto one another, even when their footprints differ. That way you build one stack that moves as a single unit.

### How much weight can the PACKOUT trolley box carry?
According to Milwaukee, the PACKOUT trolley box has a load capacity of up to 113 kg. In practice, keep the heavy boxes at the bottom and stay within the limit, because the stack still has to go up steps and ramps.

### Are PACKOUT tool boxes waterproof?
PACKOUT tool boxes and the trolley box carry an IP65 seal. In plain words, dust cannot get in and the box is protected against water jets, such as heavy rain. They are not designed to sit submerged in water, though.

### Can the drawers open with boxes stacked on top?
Yes. According to Milwaukee, the drawers on PACKOUT drawer boxes open even with boxes stacked on top. Each drawer runs on steel ball-bearing slides and holds up to 11 kg, and a locking bar keeps them shut in transit.

### What is the ONE-KEY TICK mount on PACKOUT boxes?
It is a dedicated mounting spot for the ONE-KEY TICK tracker. With it, the box can be registered on Milwaukee's ONE-KEY platform, the free tool inventory and tracking platform.
