**Short answer:** Ah shows capacity: how much work a Milwaukee battery delivers per charge. The HIGH OUTPUT and FORGE tiers are about something else: how much current the battery supplies under load and how hot it runs. For impact drivers and drills a standard REDLITHIUM pack is usually enough; for grinders, rotary hammers and saws, HIGH OUTPUT or FORGE is worth it.

The M18 battery shelf is full of codes: B, HB, FB and numbers from 2 to 12. The confusion is understandable, because two different things are mixed into the name: capacity and tier. Let's separate them, using data from Milwaukee's official pages.

## What does Ah mean on a battery?

Amp-hours (Ah) are the capacity, the battery's "fuel tank". The higher the number, the more work you get done before you need to recharge. A 5.0 Ah battery lasts longer than a 2.0 Ah one in the same tool.

Ah does not tell you how much power the tool gets when it is pushed hard, though. A grinder cutting or a rotary hammer drilling concrete draws a lot of current. That is where the battery tier matters.

## What are the three M18 battery tiers?

Milwaukee divides its M18 batteries into three levels:

- **REDLITHIUM (core):** the B batteries, such as the [M18 B5](/en/proion/bataria-18v-5-0ah-m18-b5-4932430483-4932430483). They cover most everyday work.
- **HIGH OUTPUT (upgraded):** the HB batteries, such as the [M18 HB3](/en/proion/bataria-li-on-m18-hb3-3-0ah-4932471069-4932471069), for more power and lower temperatures under load.
- **FORGE (maximum performance):** the FB6, FB8 and FB12. According to Milwaukee, this is the tier with the most power, the fastest charging and the longest life within the REDLITHIUM range.

All three fit every M18 tool. FORGE uses tabless cell technology and, according to Milwaukee, has 50% lower electrical resistance and 45% less heat.

### What does a HIGH OUTPUT pack like the M18 HB5 offer?

The M18 HB5 is a good example of the tier. According to Milwaukee, compared with the 5.0 Ah M18 B5, it:

- delivers up to 50% more power and runs up to 50% cooler,
- does up to 50% more work in medium- and high-demand applications,
- charges up to 35% faster on Super Chargers,
- has roughly the same size and weight, at 0.785 kg.

It uses tabless 18650 cells and has a fuel gauge you can read in sunlight.

### What does a FORGE pack like the M18 FB12 offer?

The M18 FB12 is the 12.0 Ah FORGE battery. According to Milwaukee, it delivers up to 50% more power than the M18 HIGH OUTPUT 12.0 Ah. It has COOL-CYCLE active cooling and a housing that resists oils, solvents and greases. It weighs 1.51 kg.

## What do you gain from REDLITHIUM technology?

Milwaukee compares REDLITHIUM batteries with conventional lithium batteries and quotes up to 40% more run-time, 20% more power and 50% more recharges.

Two more points matter in practice on site:

- **Cold:** according to the M18 page, conventional batteries stop at around -6 °C, while REDLITHIUM keeps going down to -20 °C.
- **Overheating:** extra-large welding tabs reduce resistance and move heat to the housing. If the battery gets too hot, the REDLINK PLUS electronics shut it off to protect it. Once it cools down, it works again.

On the M12 page Milwaukee also lists shock-absorbing separators, a one-piece cell carrier and a rubber overmould.

## Which batteries are there for M12?

All M12 REDLITHIUM batteries work with all current M12 tools. The core packs are the M12 B2, B3, B4 and B6. For the M12 FUEL range Milwaukee developed the HIGH OUTPUT M12 HB2.5 and [M12 HB5 HIGH OUTPUT](/en/proion/bataria-li-on-m12-hb5-5ah-4932480165-4932480165). M12 batteries do not fit M18 tools.

## Which battery for which tool?

This table is practical guidance from us, not a Milwaukee specification. Any M18 battery will work in any M18 tool; the right choice simply makes the tool more productive or lighter.

| Tool / use | Suggested tier | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Impact driver, drill driver for screwing | low-capacity REDLITHIUM or small HIGH OUTPUT | light package in the hand |
| Percussion drill in wood and brick | mid-capacity REDLITHIUM or HIGH OUTPUT | balance of run-time and weight |
| 125 mm angle grinder, SDS-Plus rotary hammer, reciprocating saw | HIGH OUTPUT or FORGE | high current, less heat |
| 230 mm grinder and continuous heavy cutting all day | high-capacity FORGE | more power and run-time |
| Site lights, radio, low-draw tools | high-capacity REDLITHIUM | run-time matters, not power |
| M12 FUEL tools | M12 HB2.5 or M12 HB5 | developed for the M12 FUEL range |

*Practical recommendation from the store, not Milwaukee data.*

## How do you keep batteries in good condition?

General advice that applies to most lithium batteries:

- Do not leave batteries in a closed car in the sun in summer.
- For long storage, keep them charged, not completely flat, in a cool, dry place.
- If a battery shuts down from overheating, let it cool before putting it on the charger.
- Keep the contacts clean of dust and grease.
- Use a charger from the same platform or an M12-18 dual-platform charger.
