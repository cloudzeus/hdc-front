**Short answer:** MX FUEL is Milwaukee's battery system for light site equipment, with more than 25 solutions: cut-off saws, breakers, concrete vibrators, lighting, pumps. According to Milwaukee, it aims to match the performance of petrol, corded and pneumatic machines, with no exhaust fumes, so it can also work indoors, in trenches and in tunnels.

On a building site there is a class of machines that for years needed petrol, a cable or a compressor: the saw for kerbstones, the heavy breaker for demolition, the vibrator for the concrete pour, the light tower for the night shift. MX FUEL is Milwaukee's battery-powered answer to that class.

## What is the Milwaukee MX FUEL system?

MX FUEL is a separate Milwaukee platform for light equipment, with its own MX FUEL REDLITHIUM battery. The company counts more than 25 solutions in it. The goal, as Milwaukee describes it, is the same performance as petrol, corded and pneumatic equipment, with zero emissions.

This is not about drill drivers and impact drivers. It is about machines that until now travelled with a fuel can next to them.

## Why battery instead of a petrol engine?

Milwaukee builds the case for MX FUEL on a few practical advantages:

- **No exhaust fumes:** the machines can work indoors, in trenches, in tunnels and in mines.
- **Lower noise and vibration hazards:** the company reports a reduction in both.
- **No petrol-engine maintenance:** there is no combustion engine to tune or send in for servicing.
- **No refuelling and no pull-start:** you do not carry fuel and you do not yank a cord to get it going.

In practice this means a crew can cut or break inside a basement or an occupied building without setting up ventilation for exhaust gases.

## What is inside an MX FUEL machine?

As with FUEL tools, Milwaukee combines three elements of its own:

1. **A POWERSTATE brushless motor**, for power without carbon brushes.
2. **REDLINK PLUS electronics**, which manage communication between machine and battery.
3. **An MX FUEL REDLITHIUM battery**, designed for the MX FUEL system.

## What do MX FUEL FORGE batteries change?

MX FUEL FORGE batteries, such as the HD812 and XC608, are the top tier of the platform. According to Milwaukee:

- the HD812 delivers up to 2x run-time,
- the FORGE range charges up to 2x faster,
- they support COOL-CYCLE active cooling,
- they resist oils, greases and solvents, with a patented ingress-protection coating,
- they offer an increased cycle life.

One figure Milwaukee gives from its own runtime data: up to 50 cuts in kerbstone on the MX FUEL FORGE 12.0 Ah battery.

### What does the MXF COS350G2 cut-off saw offer?

The MXF COS350G2 takes a 350 mm blade and cuts up to 125 mm deep. The anti-kickback system limits sudden kickback of the machine and the RAPIDSTOP brake stops the blade in under 2 seconds, according to Milwaukee. With no exhaust fumes, it can also cut indoors.

### What can the MXF DH2528H breaker do?

Milwaukee presents it as the first cordless breaker with a 28 mm Hex fitting. On one MXF XC608 battery it removes, according to the company, up to 2.5 tonnes of concrete or digs a trench up to 18 m long, 20 cm deep and 30 cm wide.

Its vibration level of 5.17 m/s² allows, according to Milwaukee, more than 7 hours of work before the operator reaches the hand-arm vibration (HAVS) exposure limit. A service light comes on every 40 hours for greasing, and an optional transport trolley is available.

## What does ONE-KEY add to MX FUEL machines?

Both the MXF COS350G2 and the MXF DH2528H have ONE-KEY. It is Milwaukee's free, cloud-based platform for equipment tracking and inventory. From a computer, tablet or phone you can follow your equipment, set geofences, receive alerts and remotely lock a machine that has gone missing. For machines that stay on site for days, that has real practical value.

## When is it worth switching to MX FUEL?

In our view, MX FUEL is a particularly good fit when:

- you work in basements, enclosed buildings or renovations in spaces that stay in use,
- you dig trenches or work inside excavations and tunnels,
- you want to stop transporting and storing fuel,
- you care about your crew's exposure time to vibration.

Whichever machine you choose, cutting and demolition produce dust and debris. Suitable [personal protective equipment](/en/katalogos/mesa-atomikis-prostasias-odopoiia) (eye, hand, hearing and respiratory protection) is not optional.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Milwaukee MX FUEL?
It is Milwaukee's battery system for light site equipment, with more than 25 solutions such as cut-off saws, breakers, concrete vibrators, lighting and pumps. According to Milwaukee, it aims to match the performance of petrol, corded and pneumatic equipment, with no exhaust fumes.

### Can I use MX FUEL equipment indoors?
Yes. Because it runs on batteries and produces no exhaust fumes, Milwaukee positions it for indoor work, trenches, tunnels and mines as well. Dust from cutting or demolition is still there, so personal protective equipment is needed as usual.

### How much work does the MXF DH2528H do on one battery?
According to Milwaukee, on one MXF XC608 battery the MXF DH2528H breaker removes up to 2.5 tonnes of concrete or digs a trench up to 18 metres long, 20 cm deep and 30 cm wide.

### How quickly does the blade stop on the MXF COS350G2 cut-off saw?
According to Milwaukee, the RAPIDSTOP brake stops the blade in under 2 seconds. The saw also has an anti-kickback system.

### Does MX FUEL equipment need maintenance?
It does not need petrol-engine maintenance, refuelling or pull-starting. It still needs basic care: on the MXF DH2528H, for example, a service light comes on every 40 hours as a reminder to grease it.
