**Short answer:** For an impact driver, choose SHOCKWAVE bits, which Milwaukee designs for impact use, with a hardened WEAR GUARD TIP and a SHOCK ZONE that absorbs load peaks. For concrete, SDS-Plus covers lighter rotary hammers (MX4 from 3.5 to 32 mm) and SDS-Max the heavier ones (MX4 from 12 to 52 mm).

A good tool only performs as well as its accessory allows. A bit that chews up on the second screw, or a drill bit that loses its diameter in concrete, costs time and forces you to redo the job. Below we explain what to look for in impact driver bits and SDS drill bits.

## Why do standard bits break in an impact driver?

An impact driver does not turn smoothly like a drill driver. When the screw resists, it delivers a series of rotational blows. Each blow is a sharp torque peak that passes straight through the bit.

A standard bit is not built for this. If it is very hard and brittle, it cracks and snaps. If it is soft, the tip wears, starts slipping out of the screw and damages the screw head. Either way you lose time and screws.

## What makes SHOCKWAVE bits different?

Milwaukee's SHOCKWAVE IMPACT DUTY line is designed for impact drivers and heavy impact use. It covers both fastening and drilling. According to Milwaukee:

- **WEAR GUARD TIP:** the tip is laser-hardened so it resists wear and keeps a proper fit in the screw throughout its life.
- **SHOCK ZONE:** part of the body flexes like a spring and absorbs the peaks of the blows, reducing tip breakage.
- **Special heat treatment and steel** developed by Milwaukee itself.
- **Sizes and sets:** hex bits from 2.5 to 12 mm, plus ready-made sets.

## When do you need SDS-Plus and when SDS-Max?

SDS-Plus and SDS-Max are two different chuck systems for rotary hammers. They are not interchangeable: the bit must match the tool's chuck.

- **SDS-Plus:** lighter hammers, for plugs, anchors, cable and pipe runs, small and medium holes. One example is the M18 ONEFHPX: according to Milwaukee it delivers 5.0 J of impact energy, drills up to 32 mm in concrete and works best in the 8–24 mm range.
- **SDS-Max:** heavier hammers, for large diameters, deep holes and serious chiselling.

If most of your holes fall within the optimal range of an SDS-Plus hammer, that is enough. If you often drill large diameters in reinforced concrete, look at SDS-Max. You will find all models among our [cordless rotary hammers](/en/katalogos/peristrofika-skaptika-pistoleta).

## What are Milwaukee MX4 4-cutter drill bits?

MX4 bits are concrete drill bits with a four-cutter carbide head, in SDS-Plus and SDS-Max versions.

### SDS-Plus MX4

- fully welded solid carbide head up to Ø20 mm; above Ø20, a one-piece slotted carbide head,
- 4-cutter head with a sharp centering tip, rebar protection and dust grooves,
- alternating SPEED and HEAVY-DUTY spirals with a thicker core,
- convex shank end for better energy transfer,
- PGM certification for anchor-setting accuracy, made in Germany.

### SDS-Max MX4

- large carbide head with chamfers for rebar,
- symmetrical 4 x 90° head and 130° centering point,
- robust secondary cutters and short spirals for fast dust removal,
- extra carbide above Ø32 mm and a reinforced flute,
- wear marks on the primary cutter, so you can check whether the bit still holds its diameter,
- PGM conformity.

## Why pre-drill with a short bit first?

Milwaukee notes that for the longest lengths you should first start the hole with a short bit of the same diameter. A very long bit wanders at the start of the hole and struggles to stay straight. With the pilot from the short bit, the long one goes in straight and is under less strain.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use standard bits in an impact driver?
They fit, but they are not made for the constant blows of an impact driver, so they wear or break sooner. Milwaukee designs SHOCKWAVE bits for impact use, with a hardened WEAR GUARD TIP and a SHOCK ZONE that absorbs load peaks.

### What is the difference between SDS-Plus and SDS-Max?
They are two different chuck systems. SDS-Plus covers lighter rotary hammers and small to medium diameters, SDS-Max heavier hammers and large diameters. In Milwaukee's MX4 range, SDS-Plus bits run from 3.5 to 32 mm and SDS-Max bits from 12 to 52 mm. The two systems are not interchangeable.

### What does a "4-cutter" drill bit mean?
It means the bit head has four carbide cutting edges. On SDS-Max MX4 bits Milwaukee lists a symmetrical 4 x 90° head with a 130° centering point, and on SDS-Plus MX4 bits a 4-cutter head with a sharp centering tip.

### What is PGM certification on concrete drill bits?
It concerns hole accuracy, so that plugs and anchors are set correctly. Milwaukee lists PGM certification for SDS-Plus MX4 bits and PGM conformity for SDS-Max MX4 bits.

### What diameter can a cordless SDS-Plus hammer drill?
It depends on the model. For example, for the M18 ONEFHPX Milwaukee lists 5.0 J, a maximum of 32 mm in concrete and an optimal range of 8–24 mm. For larger diameters, look at the SDS-Max system.
