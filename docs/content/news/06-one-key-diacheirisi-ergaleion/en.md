**Short answer:** ONE-KEY is Milwaukee's connected tool platform, which the company presents as the first of its kind. It is free and cloud-based: you log your tools in a digital inventory, see where they are, receive alerts, remotely lock ONE-KEY tools and change their settings from a phone, tablet or computer.

In a company with three crews, the question "where is the impact wrench?" costs hours every week. One tool gets left behind on a jobsite, another is lent out and never comes back. ONE-KEY brings all of that onto one screen, without paperwork or phone calls.

## What is Milwaukee ONE-KEY?

ONE-KEY is two things at once. On one side, a free cloud-based inventory and tracking platform where you organise tools, equipment and teams. On the other, a technology built into specific Milwaukee tools that connects them to the platform.

Milwaukee describes it as a way to control your tools, team and jobsite from any device. You manage it from a computer, tablet or smartphone, and information can be shared with staff in the field.

## What can you do with ONE-KEY?

The table below sums up the features Milwaukee lists and the practical problem each one solves.

| Feature | What it solves in practice |
|---|---|
| Digital inventory (dashboard) | You see on one screen which tools the business owns |
| Item details | Each tool has its own record with its information |
| Tool history | You know where a tool has been and what happened to it |
| Service reminders | Maintenance happens on time, not after something breaks |
| Transfers between jobs | It is logged which tool went to which job |
| Barcode scanning | You identify barcoded items quickly |
| Community Bluetooth tracking | Helps locate a tool that is missing |
| Geofencing and real-time alerts | You learn early that something is not where it should be |
| Remote locking | A lost or stolen ONE-KEY tool will not run |
| Tool customisation | You adjust connected tools to suit the job |
| Reporting (green rotary laser) | Logs tool activity and an accuracy protocol |

*Features as described by Milwaukee Europe on its ONE-KEY and product pages. The "What it solves" column is our own practical interpretation.*

## How does ONE-KEY help prevent tool loss?

Loss prevention rests on three layers.

- **Tracking:** community Bluetooth tracking and geofencing give you a picture of where your equipment is.
- **Alerts:** real-time alerts let you know before a problem grows.
- **Locking:** on ONE-KEY tools you can activate remote locking. A locked tool will not run, so it is of no use to whoever took it.

There is also an option for equipment without built-in ONE-KEY: PACKOUT tool boxes have a dedicated mounting spot for the ONE-KEY TICK tracker. That way the tool box itself can be logged too.

## Who benefits most from ONE-KEY?

- **Companies with crews on several sites:** they see which tool is on which job and log every transfer.
- **Tool rental businesses:** history and service reminders keep the fleet in order.
- **Maintenance teams** in factories, hotels or shipyards: they know what they have, where it is and when it needs checking.
- **The sole tradesperson:** at zero platform cost they gain a tool history, reminders and the option to lock a tool if something goes missing from the van.

## What should you know before you start?

A few practical points make the difference:

1. **Account:** you create an account in the app, and that is where registration begins.
2. **Initial setup:** set aside a day to enter all your tools. The more complete the inventory, the more useful it is.
3. **Roles:** decide who in the team can view and who can edit records.
4. **Transparency:** let your co-workers know the tools are tracked. The aim is the equipment, not the people.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does the ONE-KEY platform cost anything?
According to Milwaukee, the ONE-KEY cloud platform is free of charge. You create an account in the app, register your tools and from there manage your inventory from a computer, tablet or phone.

### What can I do if a ONE-KEY tool is lost or stolen?
You can lock the ONE-KEY tool remotely so that it will not run. The platform also offers community Bluetooth tracking, geofencing and real-time alerts, which Milwaukee intends for loss prevention. No system can guarantee the tool will be found.

### What is geofencing in ONE-KEY?
It is a virtual boundary around a location, for example a jobsite or your warehouse. Combined with the platform's alerts, it helps you notice quickly when something is not where it should be.

### How can I tell whether a Milwaukee tool has ONE-KEY?
On many models the name starts with ONE, such as the M18 ONEFHPX or the M18 ONEID3. There are exceptions, though, such as the M18 FSAGSVO125X safety grinder, so the reliable answer is in the product description.

### Is ONE-KEY worth it for a sole tradesperson?
Usually yes, since the platform is free. Even with a few tools you keep a history and service reminders, and you can remotely lock a ONE-KEY tool if it goes missing from the van.
