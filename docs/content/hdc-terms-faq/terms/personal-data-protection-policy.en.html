Protecting your personal data matters to us. This policy explains in plain words what data the Milwaukee Heavy Duty Centre online store collects, why, who we share it with and what your rights are under the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Greek Law 4624/2019.

1. Controller

ΑΦΟΙ ΚΟΛΛΕΡΗ ΙΚΕ (AFOI KOLLERI IKE), VAT no. 099095556, K. Mavromichali 4, 185 45 Piraeus, Greece. Phone +30 210 422 02 39, email info@kolleris.com.

2. What we collect

  • Contact and delivery details: name, address, postcode, phone, email.
  • Order details: products, amounts, shipping and payment method, order history.
  • Invoicing details (if you ask for an invoice): company name, VAT number, tax office, business activity, address.
  • Account details (if you create an account): email and password – the password is stored only in hashed form.
  • Payment details: only the outcome of the transaction. Card details are seen and processed solely by Viva Payments; we never see or store them.
  • Communication with us: whatever you send us by email, phone or form.
  • Newsletter: your email, only if you subscribe and confirm.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, cookies (see the Cookie policy below).

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Fulfilling your order, delivery, pickup, returns and customer service – performance of a contract.
  • Issuing sales documents and keeping accounts – legal obligation.
  • Newsletter and offers – consent, which you can withdraw at any time via the link in every email.
  • Visitor statistics (Google Analytics) – consent, through the cookie banner.
  • Ad measurement and remarketing (Google Ads) – consent, through the cookie banner.
  • Store security and fraud prevention – legitimate interest.

We do not make decisions about you solely by automated means. Advertising based on your visits to the store (remarketing) happens only if you accept it in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw it at any time from "Cookie settings".

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only as needed with partners who are contractually bound to protect it:

  • Viva Payments (Vivabank) – payments.
  • ACS Courier – parcel delivery.
  • Our hosting, email delivery and business management (ERP) providers.
  • Google (Google Analytics and Google Ads) – statistics and advertising, only if you accept them.
  • Accountants and public authorities where required by law (e.g. e-invoicing is transmitted to the Greek tax authority, AADE).

Some providers, such as Google, may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, transfers rely on GDPR safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Orders and sales documents: as long as tax and commercial law requires.
  • Account: while it is active; if you delete it, we keep only what the law obliges us to.
  • Communication: as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

After that, data is deleted or anonymised.

6. Security

We protect data with an encrypted connection (HTTPS), controlled access to our systems and reliable providers. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we keep reviewing and improving our measures.

7. Minors

The store is not aimed at people under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise them, write to info@kolleris.com; we will reply within one month.

If you believe your rights have been infringed, you may lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Kifisias 1-3, 115 23 Athens, www.dpa.gr).

9. Changes

If anything changes in how we process data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
