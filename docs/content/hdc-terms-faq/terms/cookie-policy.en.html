Cookies are small files your browser stores when you visit a website. The Milwaukee Heavy Duty Centre online store uses as few as possible; here is exactly which ones and what each does.

1. Essential cookies

The store cannot work without them (cart, sign-in, language), so they do not need your consent. They are not used for tracking.

CookiePurposeDuration
HDC_CARTKeeps your cart30 days
HDC_SESSIONKeeps you signed in to your account30 days
HDC_LOCALERemembers your language1 year
HDC_TKRemembers your postcode to calculate shipping30 days
HDC_PLATFORMRemembers the battery platform you chose (M12, M18, MX FUEL)1 year
HDC_COMPAREKeeps the products you added to compare7 days

2. Statistics cookies (only with your consent)

If you accept them in the banner ("Accept all", or "Statistics" in the settings), we use Google Analytics to see, in aggregate and anonymously, which pages are read and where visitors get stuck, so we can improve the store. If you decline, no statistics cookies are set.

CookieProviderPurposeDuration
_ga, _ga_*GoogleDistinguish visits for statisticsup to 2 years

3. Advertising cookies (only with your consent)

If you accept them in the banner ("Accept all", or "Advertising" in the settings), we use Google Ads to measure which of our ads bring visits and purchases, and to show you relevant ads for the store on other websites (remarketing). If you decline, no advertising cookies are set and Google receives no data for personalised advertising.

CookieProviderPurposeDuration
_gcl_*GoogleLink a click on an ad to the visit or purchase that follows90 days
IDE (doubleclick.net)GoogleRemarketing and ad measurementup to 13 months

4. Third-party cookies during payment

When you pay by card or IRIS you are taken to Viva Payments' secure page, where Viva uses its own cookies, necessary for the security of the transaction, under its own policy.

5. Changing your choice

Your answer to the banner is stored in your browser, not in a cookie, for 12 months; after that we ask again. To change or withdraw your consent, press "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page or at the top of this section. The change applies immediately. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings; if you block the essential ones, the cart and sign-in will not work.
