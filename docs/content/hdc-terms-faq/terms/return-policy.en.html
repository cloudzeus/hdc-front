We want you to be sure about every purchase. If a product is not right for you, you can return it as described below and in line with Greek and EU distance-selling law (Law 2251/1994, as in force).

1. Right of withdrawal (14 days)

As a consumer you may withdraw from your purchase without giving a reason within 14 calendar days of the day you received the product (from the courier or at our store).

2. How to start a return

  1. Email info@kolleris.com or call +30 210 422 02 39 with your order number (HDC-…) and the items you are returning. You may use the model withdrawal form below, but you do not have to.
  2. We will reply with instructions for sending the item back.
  3. Send or bring the item within 14 days of your notice. Returning it to our store (K. Mavromichali 4, Piraeus) during opening hours costs you nothing.

3. Condition of the product

You may examine the product as you would in a shop, but please do not use it for work. Return it with all parts, batteries, charger, case and documents it came with, ideally in its original packaging. If the product has lost value because it was handled beyond what was needed to examine it, we may deduct that loss from the refund.

4. Return costs

You pay the cost of sending the item back, unless it is faulty or we sent you the wrong item – then we cover it.

5. Refund

We refund what you paid, including the original standard delivery cost, within 14 days of your withdrawal notice, using the same payment method unless we agree otherwise. We may wait until we receive the item or until you show us that you have sent it. If you chose ACS Express, we refund the cost of standard delivery (ACS Courier).

6. Exceptions

  • Products made or customised to your specifications.
  • Sealed goods that cannot be returned for health or hygiene reasons once unsealed after delivery (e.g. ear plugs).

7. Faulty or wrong item

If your item arrived faulty, damaged or different from what you ordered, tell us as soon as possible – ideally within 2 working days, with photos if the packaging was visibly damaged. We will replace, repair or refund it under the legal guarantee and cover all shipping costs.

8. Purchases with an invoice

The statutory right of withdrawal applies to consumers. For purchases invoiced for business use, please contact us and we will consider each return request individually.
